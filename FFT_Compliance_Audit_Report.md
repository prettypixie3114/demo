## 📌 KEY METRICS GLOSSARY

- **ROAS (Return on Ad Spend):** Revenue generated ÷ Ad spend. Target: 1.5x = ₹1.50 revenue per ₹1 spent
- **CPC (Cost Per Click):** Total spend ÷ Link clicks. Target: ₹25-35
- **CTR (Click-Through Rate):** Clicks ÷ Impressions × 100. Industry target: ≥1.2%
- **CPM (Cost Per 1,000 Impressions):** (Spend ÷ Impressions) × 1,000. Benchmark: ₹100-150
- **Compliance Score:** Percentage of metrics meeting protocol requirements
